# On-Call Compensation Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy outlines the compensation structure and expectations for folder operations staff assigned to on-call shifts at Larkspur Freight. The policy applies to all full-time and part-time folder operations employees in the Dallas, Tacoma, and Savannah offices. On-call shifts are defined as any period outside of standard business hours (6:00 PM to 6:00 AM local time, weekends, and public holidays) during which employees are required to be available to respond to operational incidents, system alerts, or urgent vendor requests (e.g., from CargoSync or FleetPulse).

## Eligibility and Assignment

Eligibility for on-call compensation is limited to folder operations associates and supervisors who have completed at least 90 days of service. On-call assignments are scheduled monthly by the Operations Scheduling Coordinator (currently: Jamie Tran, Dallas office) using the ShiftMaster tool. Employees may not be assigned more than 8 on-call shifts per month, and must have at least 24 hours off between on-call periods. Requests for exemption due to medical or personal reasons must be submitted to HR (hr@larkspurfreight.com) at least 10 business days in advance.

## Compensation Structure

On-call compensation is structured as follows:
- Standby Pay: $45 USD per on-call shift (12 hours), regardless of whether the employee is called in.
- Call-In Pay: If called in, employees receive an additional $30 USD per hour, with a minimum of 1 hour per incident.
- For holidays (as defined in the Larkspur Freight Holiday Calendar), standby pay increases to $65 USD per shift.
- Tacoma office employees receive a 10% premium on all on-call pay due to regional cost-of-living adjustments.
- All payments are processed via the PayFlex system and included in the next regular payroll cycle.

## Expectations and Responsibilities

Employees on-call must:
- Respond to all alerts from the OpsAlert system within 15 minutes.
- Be reachable by phone and Slack (LarkspurOps channel) at all times during the shift.
- Log all incidents and actions in the IncidentTracker portal within 2 hours of resolution.
- Coordinate with external vendors (e.g., CargoSync, FleetPulse) as needed.
Failure to meet these expectations may result in forfeiture of on-call pay for the affected shift, subject to review by the Operations Manager (currently: Priya Desai, Savannah office).

## Reporting and Approval Process

All on-call hours and incidents must be reported in the IncidentTracker portal by 10:00 AM the following business day. Supervisors are responsible for reviewing and approving entries within 48 hours. Discrepancies or disputes regarding hours or pay must be submitted to payroll@larkspurfreight.com within 5 business days of the pay date. The final approver for all on-call compensation is the Regional Operations Director (currently: Marcus Bell, Dallas office).

## Exceptions and Special Cases

Employees who swap on-call shifts must notify the Scheduling Coordinator and update the ShiftMaster tool at least 48 hours in advance. Emergency swaps (less than 48 hours) require approval from the Operations Manager. In cases of system-wide outages (as declared by IT via the StatusBoard tool), all on-call staff will receive double call-in pay for the duration of the outage. Any exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by the Regional Operations Director.

## Review and Policy Updates

This policy is reviewed annually each March by the HR and Operations teams. Updates are communicated via email and posted in the LarkspurOps Slack channel. Employees are responsible for reviewing policy changes. Questions regarding this policy should be directed to HR (contact: Melissa Yoon, HR Manager, Savannah office). The next scheduled review is March 2025.
