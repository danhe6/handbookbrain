Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

1. Eligibility and Scope

All full-time employees at Larkspur Freight are eligible for Paid Time Off (PTO) benefits beginning on their first day of employment. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO at 50% of the full-time rate. Temporary and contract staff are not eligible for PTO. PTO policies apply to all U.S. offices (Dallas HQ, Seattle, and Atlanta), with some local variations outlined below. Employees must be classified as active and not on unpaid leave to accrue PTO.

2. PTO Accrual Rates

PTO accrues on a bi-weekly basis and is reflected in the WorkNest HR portal. Accrual rates by office:

Employees with 5+ years of service receive an additional 3 days/year. Accrual is capped at 1.5x the annual rate (e.g., Dallas max: 22.5 days).

  • Dallas HQ: 15 days/year (0.577 days per pay period)
  • Seattle: 18 days/year (0.692 days per pay period)
  • Atlanta: 12 days/year (0.462 days per pay period)

3. PTO Carryover and Forfeiture

Unused PTO may be carried over into the next calendar year, subject to office-specific limits:

Any PTO above these limits as of January 31st will be forfeited. No cash payout for unused PTO except upon separation, per Section 7.

  • Dallas HQ: Up to 7 days
  • Seattle: Up to 10 days
  • Atlanta: Up to 5 days

4. PTO Request Process

All PTO requests must be submitted via the WorkNest HR portal at least 10 business days in advance for planned absences. For unplanned absences (e.g., illness), notify your direct supervisor and log the absence in WorkNest within 24 hours. PTO requests are reviewed and approved by the employee’s department manager. Approval is subject to business needs and staffing levels. Employees are encouraged to coordinate with their teams to minimize disruption.

5. Blackout Dates and Peak Periods

Certain periods are designated as blackout dates due to high operational demand (e.g., December 10–31, June 1–15 for Dallas and Atlanta). PTO during these times requires VP-level approval. Seattle office does not have blackout dates but may restrict PTO during the annual Port Summit (first week of May).

6. Tracking and Reporting

PTO balances are updated daily in the WorkNest HR portal. Employees can view their current balance, accrual rate, and pending requests at any time. HR will send monthly PTO balance summaries via email. Discrepancies should be reported to hr@larkspurfreight.com within 30 days. Supervisors are responsible for monitoring team PTO usage to ensure adequate coverage.

7. PTO Payout Upon Separation

Upon voluntary or involuntary separation, accrued but unused PTO will be paid out in the final paycheck, up to the annual accrual cap for the employee’s office. For example, a Dallas employee may receive payout for up to 22.5 days. Employees terminated for gross misconduct forfeit all unused PTO. Final PTO payout is processed through our payroll vendor, PayBridge Solutions.
