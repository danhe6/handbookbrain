Data Retention Policy

1. Purpose and Scope

This Data Retention Policy outlines the retention periods, deletion schedules, and responsibilities for managing company data at Larkspur Freight. The policy applies to all digital and physical records generated or received by Larkspur Freight employees, contractors, and third-party vendors, including but not limited to shipment logs, customer contracts, HR files, and financial records. The policy covers all offices: Chicago HQ, Dallas Branch, and Seattle Logistics Center. Compliance is mandatory for all staff, and non-compliance may result in disciplinary action or termination of vendor contracts.

2. Retention Periods by Data Type

Retention periods are reviewed annually by the IT Security Manager (currently: Priya Desai).

  • Shipment records: 5 years from date of delivery (Chicago HQ), 4 years (Dallas Branch), 6 years (Seattle Logistics Center)
  • Customer contracts: 7 years after contract end date
  • Employee HR files: 6 years after termination
  • Financial records: 8 years from fiscal year end
  • Email correspondence: 2 years (all offices)
  • CCTV footage: 30 days (HQ), 14 days (Dallas), 60 days (Seattle)

3. Deletion Schedules and Automation

Data deletion is scheduled monthly on the 15th using the DataSweep Pro tool (vendor: ByteHarbor Solutions). Automated scripts flag eligible records for deletion, and a manual review is conducted by the Data Compliance Officer (currently: Marcus Lin). Exceptions require written approval from the department head. Physical records are shredded quarterly by SecureShred Inc. (invoice avg: $1,200/quarter).

4. Backup and Archival Procedures

Backups are retained for 90 days on encrypted cloud storage (vendor: CloudNest). After 90 days, backups are purged unless flagged for legal hold. Archived data (older than 5 years) is stored on LTO-9 tapes in the Chicago HQ vault, with an annual storage cost of $3,800. Only IT Security and Legal teams have access to archives. Restoration requests must be approved by the CIO (currently: Elena Voss).

5. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

Retention schedules comply with FMCSA, IRS, and state-specific regulations. For example, Texas DOT requires shipment logs to be kept for a minimum of 4 years. Legal holds override standard retention periods; Legal Counsel (currently: David Kim) must notify IT within 2 business days of a hold. Non-compliance fines can reach up to $50,000 per incident.

6. Data Subject Requests and Exceptions

Data subjects (employees, customers) may request deletion or access to their data under the Larkspur Privacy Policy. Requests are processed within 30 days. Exceptions to retention periods (e.g., ongoing litigation, audits) must be documented in the Data Exception Log (SharePoint: IT-Security/Exceptions). Only 3% of records are currently under exception status. All exceptions expire after 18 months unless renewed by Legal.

7. Policy Review and Updates

This policy is reviewed every 12 months by the IT Security Committee. Last review: 2023-11-10. Next scheduled review: 2024-11-15. Updates require approval from the CIO and Legal Counsel. All employees are notified of changes via company-wide email and must acknowledge receipt within 7 days. Questions should be directed to it-security@larkspurfreight.com.
