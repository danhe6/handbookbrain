# Vendor Onboarding Checklist

## 1. Initial Vendor Eligibility Screening

All new vendors must pass the eligibility screening before proceeding. The Operations team uses the VendoCheck tool to verify:
- Minimum 2 years in business (exceptions require Ops Director approval)
- Valid USD bank account (no international wires for folder ops)
- No outstanding compliance flags in the last 18 months
- For vendors in the Midwest office, a minimum annual revenue of $250,000 is required (verified via tax docs)
Screening is completed within 3 business days. Results are reviewed by the Vendor Relations Manager (currently: Dana Kim).

## 2. Documentation Collection

Vendors must submit the following documents via the Larkspur Portal:
- W-9 form (signed, dated within last 90 days)
- Certificate of Insurance (COI) with at least $1M liability coverage
- Folder Operations Service Agreement (template: FOSA-2024)
- For vendors using FleetSync, a copy of their DOT safety rating
All documents are reviewed by the Compliance Analyst (currently: Marcus Lee) within 2 business days.

## 3. Contract Review & Approval

Contracts are routed through DocuSign for e-signature. The following approvals are required:
- Operations Supervisor (East: Priya Nair, West: Tom Hensley)
- Legal review for contracts over $50,000/year (handled by LegalOps)
- Finance sign-off for payment terms exceeding Net 45
Average contract review time: 4 business days. Vendors are notified via email (from onboarding@larkspurfreight.com) once approved.

## 4. System Setup & Access

Once approved, vendors are set up in the following systems:
- Larkspur TMS (Transport Management System)
- FolderOps Dashboard (access provisioned within 24 hours)
- Payment portal (PayBridge)
Vendors receive login credentials and a setup guide. For the Dallas office, two-factor authentication is mandatory. IT Support (contact: ithelp@larkspurfreight.com) handles any access issues.

## 5. Training & Orientation

All new vendors must complete the Folder Operations Orientation (1.5 hours, virtual via Zoom). Topics include:
- Folder handling protocols
- Safety and compliance (with reference to Larkspur SOP-FO-2024)
- Use of the FolderOps Dashboard
Completion is required within 7 days of system access. Certificates are issued by the Training Coordinator (currently: Luis Ortega).

## 6. First Job Assignment & Performance Review

After orientation, vendors receive their first job assignment via the FolderOps Dashboard. Performance is reviewed after the first 3 completed jobs:
- On-time delivery rate (target: 98%)
- Compliance with folder handling SOPs
- Communication responsiveness (reply within 2 hours during business days)
Vendors not meeting standards are given a 14-day improvement period. Ongoing reviews are conducted quarterly.

## 7. Final Onboarding Confirmation

Once all steps are complete, the Vendor Relations Manager sends a final onboarding confirmation email. Vendors are added to the Approved Vendor List (AVL) and can participate in folder operations tenders. For the Chicago office, AVL updates are published every Friday by 5pm CST. Any discrepancies should be reported to vendoradmin@larkspurfreight.com within 48 hours.
