Driver Safety Manual

Introduction & Scope

This Driver Safety Manual applies to all Larkspur Freight drivers operating out of the Dallas, Memphis, and Spokane terminals. All drivers, including part-time and contract, must comply with the protocols outlined herein. The manual covers vehicle operation, incident reporting, PPE requirements, and annual training. Non-compliance may result in suspension of driving privileges for up to 14 days. Questions should be directed to the Safety Compliance Officer (currently: Marsha Lin, Dallas office).

Mandatory Safety Training

All new drivers must complete the Larkspur Safety Fundamentals course within 10 business days of hire. Annual refresher training is required every 12 months, tracked via the SafeFleet Portal (vendor: BluePine Systems). Training completion is verified by the Terminal Safety Lead. Drivers who miss the deadline will be removed from the active schedule until training is complete. Memphis office requires an additional 2-hour hazardous materials module for all drivers handling Class 3 cargo.

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • High-visibility vests (ANSI/ISEA 107-2020 compliant) are mandatory at all loading docks.
  • Steel-toe boots must be worn during all loading/unloading activities.
  • Spokane terminal issues PPE kits every 6 months (cost: $85/kit, vendor: NorthStar Supply).
  • Damaged or lost PPE must be reported within 24 hours to the Terminal Admin for replacement approval.
  • Failure to wear PPE may result in a $50 payroll deduction per incident.

Vehicle Inspection Protocols

Drivers are required to complete a pre-trip inspection checklist before each shift using the FleetCheck app (vendor: RoadLogic). The checklist includes brakes, lights, tires, and emergency equipment. Dallas drivers must submit digital inspection logs by 7:00 AM daily. Any critical defect (e.g., brake failure, tire tread < 2/32”) must be reported immediately to Fleet Maintenance (contact: Jorge Salinas). Non-critical issues must be logged within 12 hours.

Incident Reporting & Response

All accidents, regardless of severity, must be reported within 1 hour to the Safety Hotline (1-800-555-0199) and logged in the SafeFleet Portal. Memphis office requires submission of a written incident summary within 24 hours. Drug and alcohol testing is mandatory within 2 hours post-incident (testing partner: MedSure Diagnostics). Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

Fatigue Management & Hours of Service

Drivers must adhere to FMCSA Hours of Service regulations: maximum 11 hours driving after 10 consecutive hours off duty. Larkspur Freight policy prohibits more than 60 hours on duty in any 7-day period. Electronic logs are maintained via LogMaster Pro (vendor: FleetSoft). Spokane terminal enforces a mandatory 30-minute break after 6 hours of driving. Violations are reviewed weekly by the Compliance Manager (currently: T. R. McNeil).

Eligibility & Medical Requirements

  • All drivers must maintain a valid CDL and current DOT medical certificate.
  • Annual vision screening is required (cost: $45, reimbursed upon submission of receipt).
  • Drivers with more than 2 moving violations in 12 months are subject to review by the Driver Review Board.
  • Dallas office requires random drug screening for 15% of drivers each quarter (selection by SecureTest Labs).
