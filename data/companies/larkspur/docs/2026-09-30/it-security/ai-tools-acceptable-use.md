# AI Tools Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Approved AI Tools and Vendors

Larkspur Freight currently authorizes the use of the following AI assistants for business operations: CargoGenie (by BluePallet Systems), RouteOptima (by Navisight), and DocuPilot (by QuantaLogic). Use of any other AI tool, including free online chatbots, is strictly prohibited unless explicitly approved by the IT Security Manager. The list of approved tools is reviewed quarterly (next review: 15 July 2024). Any requests for additional tools must be submitted via the IT Service Portal and will be evaluated within 10 business days.

## 2. Data Classification and Usage Restrictions

Employees may only input data classified as 'Internal Use' or lower into approved AI tools. Confidential, customer-identifiable, or financial data (e.g., shipment invoices, client contracts, payroll records) must NOT be entered under any circumstances. For example, shipment tracking numbers are permitted, but customer payment details are not. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 3. Regional Office Variations

- Chicago HQ: All approved tools may be used for logistics planning and document drafting.
- Rotterdam Office: Only CargoGenie and DocuPilot are permitted; RouteOptima is pending GDPR compliance review (expected by 30 June 2024).
- Singapore Office: Use of AI tools is limited to non-customer data until local regulatory clearance is obtained (target: Q3 2024).
Employees must consult their local IT coordinator for clarifications.

## 4. Approval and Onboarding Process

Before accessing any AI assistant, employees must complete the 'AI Security Awareness' training (2 hours, available on Larkspur Academy). Department heads must submit an access request form to the IT Security Manager (currently: Priya Desai) for each user. Approval is typically granted within 5 business days. Access is reviewed annually every March.

## 5. Monitoring and Auditing

All interactions with AI tools are logged and subject to monthly audits by the IT Security team. In May 2024, 7% of users were flagged for review due to improper data entry. Employees are required to report any suspected misuse within 24 hours to security@larkspurfreight.com. Failure to comply may result in suspension of AI tool access.

## 6. Vendor Data Handling and Retention

Vendors are contractually obligated to retain Larkspur data for no longer than 30 days post-processing. BluePallet Systems and QuantaLogic provide monthly data deletion certificates. Any data breach or retention policy violation must be reported by the vendor within 48 hours. Employees should not attempt to export or download AI-generated data to personal devices.

## 7. Policy Updates and Employee Responsibilities

This policy is updated biannually, with the next revision scheduled for 1 December 2024. Employees are responsible for reviewing updates posted in the IT-Security folder on SharePoint. Questions should be directed to the IT Security Manager or local IT coordinators. Non-compliance may result in a written warning after the first offense, and further disciplinary action for repeated violations.
