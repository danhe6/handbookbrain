Corporate Card Usage
====================

Document owner: Emily Foster

ELIGIBILITY AND CARD ISSUANCE

Corporate cards are available to full-time employees in the Operations, Sales, and Logistics
departments who have completed at least 90 days of employment. Requests must be submitted
via the FinTrak portal and approved by the department head (e.g., Carla Jensen for
Operations, Mark Liu for Sales). Cards are issued by our partner, Northgate Bank, with a
standard monthly limit of $5,000 USD. Exceptions up to $10,000 require CFO approval. Cards
are not issued to contractors or part-time staff. For the Rotterdam office, only managers
are eligible for cards, with a €3,000 monthly cap.

REQUESTING A CORPORATE CARD

To request a card, employees must complete the Corporate Card Request Form in the finance
folder on SharePoint and attach a signed approval from their supervisor. The form requires:

* Employee ID and department

* Estimated monthly spend

* List of primary vendors (e.g., FleetFuel, CargoMate)

Requests are reviewed within 5 business days. For urgent needs (e.g., last-minute travel),
email finance@larkspurfreight.com with 'URGENT CARD REQUEST' in the subject. Cards are
typically delivered within 7-10 business days after approval.

PERMITTED AND PROHIBITED EXPENSES

Corporate cards may be used for:

* Approved travel (flights, hotels, taxis)

* Client meals (max $120 per meal)

* Office supplies from approved vendors (e.g., OfficeNest, SupplyBridge)

* Fuel and tolls for company vehicles

Prohibited uses include:

* Personal expenses

* Cash advances

* Alcohol purchases over $50 per event

* Gift cards

Violations may result in card suspension and disciplinary action. The Dallas office allows
ride-share apps (e.g., GoCab) up to $200/month, while the Rotterdam office does not.

TRANSACTION LIMITS AND CONTROLS

Each card has a default single-transaction limit of $2,000. Transactions above this amount
require pre-approval from the Finance Director (currently Priya Desai). Monthly spend is
capped at $5,000 unless otherwise authorized. Cards are monitored via the SpendGuard tool,
which flags:

* Transactions over $1,000

* Multiple transactions at the same vendor in 48 hours

* Weekend spending over $500

Cardholders receive automated alerts for flagged activity. Persistent overages may result in
card revocation.

EXPENSE RECONCILIATION AND RECEIPTS

All cardholders must reconcile expenses within 7 calendar days of statement close
(statements close on the 25th of each month). Receipts must be uploaded to the FinTrak
portal and matched to each transaction. Missing receipts over $50 require a written
explanation and supervisor sign-off. The finance team (contact: finance@larkspurfreight.com)
audits 10% of all cardholder statements monthly. Failure to reconcile on time may result in
a $25 late fee deducted from payroll.

LOST OR STOLEN CARDS

Report lost or stolen cards immediately to Northgate Bank at 1-800-555-0199 and notify
finance@larkspurfreight.com. Replacement cards are issued within 3 business days.
Cardholders are liable for any unauthorized charges up to $100 if not reported within 24
hours. For the Rotterdam office, contact the local finance lead, Jeroen van Dijk, for
assistance with replacements.

CARD CANCELLATION AND OFFBOARDING

Upon resignation or termination, cards must be surrendered to HR within 2 business days.
Final expense reports must be submitted within 5 days of departure. Any outstanding balances
will be deducted from final paychecks. For transfers between offices, cardholders must
reapply for a new card under the destination office’s rules. Questions about offboarding
procedures should be directed to HR (hr@larkspurfreight.com).
