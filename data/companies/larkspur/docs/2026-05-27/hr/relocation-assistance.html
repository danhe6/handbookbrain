Relocation Assistance Policy

Eligibility Criteria

Relocation assistance is available to full-time employees of Larkspur Freight who are required to move at least 50 miles from their current residence for a new or existing role. Eligibility is determined by the HR Business Partner and must be approved by the employee’s department head. Contractors, interns, and part-time staff are not eligible. Employees relocating to the Dallas or Seattle offices must have a minimum tenure of 6 months, while those moving to the Atlanta office require only 3 months. All requests must be submitted via the MoveEase portal within 10 business days of receiving the relocation offer.

Covered Expenses

The following expenses are covered under the relocation policy, up to a maximum of $7,500 per move:

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense. Any costs exceeding the cap are the employee’s responsibility.

  • Transportation of household goods (via SwiftHaul or BlueBox Movers)
  • Temporary housing for up to 21 days (booked through StayBridge Corporate)
  • One-way airfare for employee and immediate family (economy class)
  • Lease termination fees up to $1,200
  • Storage costs for up to 30 days
  • Mileage reimbursement at $0.58/mile for personal vehicle moves

Non-Covered Expenses

The following items are not reimbursable under this policy:

Employees are encouraged to review the full list of exclusions on the HR SharePoint (folder: hr/relocation). Exceptions may be considered on a case-by-case basis with written approval from the HR Director.

  • Meals during travel
  • Pet relocation fees
  • Vehicle shipping (except for company-leased vehicles)
  • Home purchase closing costs
  • Furniture purchases
  • Cleaning services

Relocation Process

To initiate relocation, employees must:

1. Submit a Relocation Request Form via MoveEase within 10 business days of offer.

2. Schedule a consultation with the assigned Relocation Coordinator (currently: Dana Kim for Dallas, Marcus Lee for Seattle, and Priya Patel for Atlanta).

3. Obtain at least two quotes from approved vendors (see vendor list on SharePoint).

4. Submit all required documentation and receipts through the MoveEase portal.

The average processing time for reimbursement is 14 business days after submission of complete documentation.

Tax Implications

Relocation reimbursements are considered taxable income under IRS guidelines. Larkspur Freight will gross up relocation payments by 22% to offset estimated tax liability. Employees will receive a summary of taxable benefits on their year-end pay statement. For questions regarding tax treatment, contact payroll@larkspurfreight.com or consult with your personal tax advisor.

Office-Specific Provisions

Dallas Office: Employees are eligible for an additional $500 stipend for utility setup costs. Seattle Office: Temporary housing may be extended to 30 days due to local housing market constraints, subject to approval by the Seattle Office Manager. Atlanta Office: Lease termination fees are reimbursed up to $1,500. All offices: Spousal job search assistance is available through CareerConnect Partners upon request.

Contacts and Support

For questions or support, contact your Relocation Coordinator or email hr@larkspurfreight.com. The HR team is available Monday–Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm CST. For urgent issues outside business hours, call the HR hotline at 1-800-555-0199. All forms and policy documents are available in the HR folder on SharePoint.
