Parental Leave Guidelines
=========================

Document owner: Marcus Lee

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

All full-time employees of Larkspur Freight who have completed at least 9 months of
continuous service are eligible for parental leave. Part-time employees working a minimum of
24 hours per week may be eligible for pro-rated leave. Contractors and temporary staff are
not eligible. Eligibility is verified through the HRIS system (PeopleNest). For
international offices:

* UK office: Employees must have worked at least 26 weeks by the 15th week before the
expected due date.

* US office: Employees must meet the federal FMLA requirements in addition to Larkspur’s
policy.

2. MATERNITY LEAVE

Maternity leave at Larkspur Freight is 16 weeks of paid leave at 100% base salary for
eligible employees. Leave can begin up to 4 weeks before the expected due date. In the UK
office, statutory maternity pay is provided after 16 weeks, up to 39 weeks total, at £172.48
per week or 90% of average weekly earnings (whichever is lower). In the US office, maternity
leave is capped at 12 weeks paid, with an optional 4 weeks unpaid extension. All maternity
leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal (PeopleNest) at least 30 days in advance.

3. PATERNITY LEAVE

Eligible employees are entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity leave at 100% base salary.
Leave must be taken within 8 weeks of the child’s birth or adoption placement. In the UK
office, statutory paternity pay is £172.48 per week for up to 2 weeks, with Larkspur topping
up to full salary for the remaining 2 weeks. In the US office, paternity leave is 4 weeks
paid, with no statutory minimum. All paternity leave requests require manager approval and
must be logged in PeopleNest at least 14 days before the intended start date.

4. ADOPTION LEAVE

Adoption leave mirrors maternity leave: 16 weeks paid at 100% base salary for primary
caregivers, and 4 weeks paid for secondary caregivers. Employees must provide official
adoption placement documentation. In the UK, statutory adoption pay applies after 16 weeks,
up to 39 weeks total. In the US, adoption leave is 12 weeks paid, with an optional 4 weeks
unpaid. All adoption leave requests must be submitted through PeopleNest and approved by the
HR Manager (currently: Dana Kessler).

5. PAY AND BENEFITS DURING LEAVE

During paid parental leave, employees continue to accrue annual leave and retain all health
and dental benefits through our provider, BluePeak Health. Pension contributions (UK) and
401(k) contributions (US) continue as normal. Bonuses and commissions are not paid during
leave periods. Payroll is processed via PayBridge, and any discrepancies should be reported
to payroll@larkspurfreight.com within 10 business days.

6. APPLICATION PROCESS

To apply for parental leave:

1. Submit a leave request in PeopleNest, including all supporting documents (such as a
medical certificate or adoption papers).

2. Notify both your direct manager and HR at least 30 days before your intended leave start
date. For paternity leave, the notice period is 14 days.

3. HR will review your request, confirm your eligibility, and send a formal approval within
7 business days.

4. If you are based in an international office, you may need to complete additional
statutory forms. Please refer to the HR intranet for specific requirements.

7. RETURN TO WORK AND FLEXIBLE ARRANGEMENTS

Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to return to their previous role or a
suitable alternative. Requests for flexible working (e.g., part-time, remote) should be
submitted via the FlexiWork tool at least 3 weeks before the planned return date. Approval
is at the discretion of the department head (US: Mark Feldman, UK: Priya Choudhury). HR will
conduct a return-to-work meeting within the first week back to discuss any support needs.
