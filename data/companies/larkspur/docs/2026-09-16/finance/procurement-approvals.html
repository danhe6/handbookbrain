Procurement Approval Matrix

Overview and Purpose

The Procurement Approval Matrix outlines the required approval levels and documentation for all vendor purchases at Larkspur Freight. This policy ensures compliance, cost control, and transparency across all offices. The matrix applies to all departments, including Operations, IT, and Fleet Management. Purchases from vendors such as Northline Tires, CargoPro Solutions, and FleetGuard Systems must follow the outlined procedures. All employees are responsible for adhering to these guidelines, and non-compliance may result in delayed payments or disciplinary action.

Approval Thresholds – Chicago Office

  • Purchases up to $2,500: Department Manager approval required.
  • $2,501–$10,000: Regional Director approval required.
  • Above $10,000: CFO (currently Linda Tran) approval required.
  • Emergency purchases (e.g., breakdowns) up to $5,000 may be pre-approved by Fleet Supervisor (notify within 24 hours).
  • All approvals must be documented in the ProcureTrack system within 2 business days.

Approval Thresholds – Dallas Office

  • Purchases up to $1,500: Team Lead approval required.
  • $1,501–$7,500: Operations Manager approval required.
  • Above $7,500: Regional VP (currently Marcus Bell) approval required.
  • All IT-related purchases, regardless of amount, require IT Manager sign-off.
  • Documentation must be uploaded to the Dallas ShareDocs folder within 3 business days.

Required Documentation

  • Vendor quote or invoice (must include vendor name, e.g., CargoPro Solutions, and itemized costs)
  • Purchase requisition form (Form PR-2024)
  • Three competitive bids for purchases above $5,000 (exceptions: sole-source vendors like FleetGuard Systems)
  • Approval email or digital signature from the designated approver
  • For recurring contracts, attach previous year’s spend summary
  • All documents must be retained for 7 years per company policy

Vendor Eligibility and Onboarding

All vendors must be approved by the Procurement Team before any purchase. New vendors (e.g., SwiftParts Inc.) require:

  • Completed Vendor Registration Form (VRF-11)
  • W-9 or equivalent tax documentation
  • Proof of insurance (minimum $1M liability)
  • Reference check (minimum 2 business references)
  • Onboarding process typically takes 5–10 business days. Exceptions for urgent needs must be approved by the CFO.

Special Categories and Exceptions

Special Categories and Exceptions:

  • Fuel purchases from approved vendors (e.g., FuelDirect) are exempt from competitive bid requirements but must be reconciled monthly.
  • Software subscriptions (e.g., FleetOps Tracker) over $2,500/year require IT and Finance joint approval.
  • Any purchase over $50,000 requires Board notification at least 10 days prior to commitment.
  • Sole-source justifications must be documented and signed by the requesting manager and Procurement Lead.

Review and Audit

The Procurement Team will conduct quarterly audits of all purchases above $5,000. Any discrepancies or missing documentation will be reported to the Finance Director (currently Priya Nair) within 7 days of discovery. Offices must respond to audit queries within 3 business days. The matrix will be reviewed annually, with updates communicated via company-wide email and posted in the Finance folder.
