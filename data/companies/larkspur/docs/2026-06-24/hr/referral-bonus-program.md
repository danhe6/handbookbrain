# Employee Referral Bonus Program

**Owner:** Derek Patel

## Program Overview

The Larkspur Freight Employee Referral Bonus Program is designed to reward current employees for referring qualified candidates who are successfully hired. The program is managed by the HR team using the TalentBridge platform. All full-time and part-time employees (excluding HR and executive leadership) are eligible to participate. The program aims to attract top talent while recognizing the valuable networks of our staff.

## Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a referral bonus:
- The referring employee must be actively employed at the time of referral and payout.
- The referred candidate must not have applied to Larkspur Freight in the past 12 months.
- Only external candidates are eligible (no current or former employees).
- HR team members, directors, and above are not eligible for bonuses.
- For the Dallas office, only referrals for operations and logistics roles qualify.

## Referral Submission Process

Referrals must be submitted via the TalentBridge portal. The submission must include:
- Candidate’s full name, contact info, and resume
- Position applied for
- Relationship to the referring employee
Submissions are timestamped and tracked by the HRIS system (PeoplePulse). Referrals must be submitted before the candidate’s first interview. Late submissions will not be considered.

## Bonus Amounts and Payout Schedule

The standard referral bonus is $1,500 (USD) for successful hires in the Chicago and Atlanta offices. For the Dallas office, the bonus is $1,000 (USD) due to local market rates. Payouts are processed in two installments:
- 50% after the new hire completes 60 days of employment
- 50% after the new hire completes 180 days of employment
Bonuses are paid via payroll and subject to standard tax withholdings.

## Approval and Exceptions

All referral bonuses must be approved by the HR Manager (currently: Dana Kim for Chicago/Atlanta, Marcus Lee for Dallas). Exceptions to eligibility or payout rules require written approval from the Director of People Operations. Disputes are reviewed monthly by the HR Compliance Committee. Appeals must be submitted within 30 days of bonus denial.

## Program Limitations and Exclusions

Employees may refer up to 5 candidates per calendar year. Bonuses are not paid for temporary, contract, or intern positions. If two employees refer the same candidate, the first submission (as recorded in TalentBridge) will be honored. Larkspur Freight reserves the right to modify or terminate the program at any time with 14 days’ notice.

## Contact and Support

For questions about the referral program, contact hr@larkspurfreight.com or submit a ticket via the PeoplePulse Helpdesk. Program details and FAQs are available in the HR folder on SharePoint. For technical issues with TalentBridge, reach out to IT support (ithelp@larkspurfreight.com).
