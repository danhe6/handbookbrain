# Warehouse Safety Procedures

## 1. Daily Safety Checks

All warehouse staff at Larkspur Freight must complete daily safety checks before starting their shift. The checklist (Form WF-12) is available at the main entrance and must be submitted to the shift supervisor by 7:15 AM. Key items include:
- Inspecting forklift brakes and lights (report issues to FleetOps within 2 hours)
- Checking fire extinguishers (minimum pressure: 120 psi)
- Ensuring all exits are clear (no obstructions within 1.5 meters)
- Verifying spill kits (at least 2 per 500 sq. meters)
Failure to submit the checklist results in a $25 deduction from the monthly safety bonus. The checklist is reviewed weekly by the Safety Coordinator, currently Jamie Lin.

## 2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements

PPE is mandatory in all warehouse zones. Standard issue includes:
- High-visibility vests (vendor: SafeVest Solutions, model SV-300)
- Steel-toe boots (minimum ASTM F2413-18 compliant)
- Gloves (cut-resistant, Level 3 or higher)
- Hard hats (required in loading bays only)
PPE must be replaced every 180 days or sooner if damaged. Replacement requests are submitted via the Larkspur Portal and approved by the Warehouse Manager (currently: Priya Desai). Non-compliance is tracked; 3 violations in 30 days result in a 1-day suspension.

## 3. Forklift Operation Protocols

Only certified operators may use forklifts. Certification is valid for 2 years and must be renewed through the Larkspur Training Center (fee: $120, reimbursed upon completion). Forklifts are equipped with FleetGuard telematics (vendor: TechFleet Inc.), which logs speed and usage. Operators must:
- Perform a pre-use inspection (log in FleetGuard app)
- Never exceed 8 km/h inside the warehouse
- Park only in designated zones (marked with yellow tape)
Any incident involving a forklift must be reported within 30 minutes to the Shift Lead and logged in the Safety Tracker.

## 4. Emergency Procedures

In case of fire, chemical spill, or medical emergency, activate the nearest alarm (located every 40 meters). Evacuation maps are posted at all exits. Assembly point for the North Office is Lot C; for the South Office, use the loading dock area. Emergency drills are held every 90 days (next scheduled: 2024-07-15). All staff must:
- Follow instructions from the Emergency Response Team (ERT)
- Do not re-enter the building until cleared by the ERT Lead (currently: Marcus O’Neill)
- Report missing persons to the ERT within 5 minutes of assembly
Failure to participate in drills results in a written warning.

## 5. Incident Reporting and Investigation

All accidents, near-misses, or unsafe conditions must be reported within 1 hour using the Incident Report Form (IRF-7) on the Larkspur Intranet. The Safety Officer (currently: Elena Varga) reviews all reports within 2 business days. For incidents resulting in injury or property damage over $500, an investigation team is assigned within 24 hours. Findings are shared in the monthly safety meeting. Repeat incidents in the same area trigger a 10% increase in random safety audits for the next 60 days.

## 6. Contractor and Visitor Safety

All contractors and visitors must sign in at reception and complete a 10-minute safety orientation video (provided by SecureEntry Media). Temporary PPE kits are available for a $15 deposit. Contractors working more than 3 consecutive days must provide proof of safety training (OSHA 10 or equivalent). Visitors are escorted at all times by a Larkspur employee. Any safety violation by a contractor results in immediate removal from site and a $200 penalty billed to the vendor.

## 7. Hazardous Materials Handling

Hazardous materials (HAZMAT) are stored in Zone D, accessible only to staff with HAZMAT Level 2 certification. Inventory is tracked using ChemSafe Pro (vendor: BlueGrid Systems). Weekly audits are conducted every Friday by the Compliance Team. Spills must be contained within 5 minutes and reported immediately. Only approved absorbents (SpillMaster 500) may be used. Disposal of hazardous waste is scheduled every 14 days with EcoTrans Disposal (cost: $350 per pickup).
